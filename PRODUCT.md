# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Romanian SME owners, administrators and office managers — largely in and around
Constanța — who carry legal obligations they are not specialists in: accounting and
tax, GDPR, SSM/PSI (health, safety and fire), payroll and HR, REGES filings, physical
security.

Their context when they arrive is rarely leisurely. They are time-poor, often mildly
anxious about an obligation they have just discovered or a deadline they have just
missed, and they are not fluent in the vocabulary of the field. They are deciding
whether to keep improvising internally or hand the whole burden to someone
accountable.

The job to be done: satisfy themselves that CESS is competent and legitimate, work out
whether it covers *their* specific obligations, and start a conversation without
committing to anything.

## Product Purpose

The public site for CESS TRUST S.R.L. (CUI 37449212), an outsourced support-services
centre covering compliance end to end.

The site exists to convert credibility into contact. It is not a brochure and not an
application — its single job is to make an unfamiliar visitor confident enough to call
or write. Success is a booked consultation; everything on the page either builds the
case for that or is in the way.

The emotional target is relief: the sense that this is handled now, by people who do it
every day.

## Brand Personality

Premium, confident, editorial.

An established firm that charges accordingly and does not need to shout about it.
Voice is plain and direct — it explains obligations in language an owner actually uses,
without either jargon or condescension. Confidence is shown through evidence
(certifications, named authorisations, real documents), not adjectives.

Editorial in the literal sense: the design carries a point of view, uses real typographic
hierarchy, and lets substantial copy breathe rather than compressing everything into
identical cards.

## Anti-references

- **Trendy startup / crypto.** Explicitly rejected. No neon accents on dark, no
  aggressive or gratuitous animation, no hype copy, no emoji, no countdown urgency.
  The audience is buying reassurance and permanence, not novelty.
- By extension: anything that reads as a template. If the layout could belong to any
  company in any sector, it is not doing its job for this one.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over adjectives.** Certificates, authorisation numbers and named
   standards do the persuading. Never claim "professional" where a document can be
   shown instead.

2. **Calm is the product.** The service removes stress, so the interface must not add
   any. Generous spacing, unhurried motion, no interruption patterns.

3. **Speak the owner's language, not the regulator's.** Name obligations the way a
   business owner encounters them. Legal precision belongs on the legal pages.

4. **Earn the premium.** The price of a serif display face and a restrained palette is
   that execution must be flawless — alignment, contrast and rhythm carry the claim.

5. **Every page is an entrance.** Visitors arrive from search on a service or legal
   page as often as on the homepage. Each one must orient a stranger and offer a way
   to make contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target: **WCAG 2.1 AA**.

- Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast; large text ≥ 3:1. This is the standard the site is most
  likely to be measured against for a public-facing Romanian business.
- Full keyboard operability with a visible focus indicator (currently a copper
  `:focus-visible` outline).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected — non-negotiable given the WebGL hero and the
  GSAP scroll work.
- Romanian-language content with correct diacritics throughout; `lang="ro"` set.
- Audience skews older and less technically confident than a typical startup audience:
  tap targets, form clarity and error recovery matter more than average.
